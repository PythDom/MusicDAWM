# R5-light report: vocal presets from DiffVox

*2026-10-31. MusicDAWM 0.1.0+ (part of the next release). Plan agreed with the user on 2026-10-31: about 10 presets, EQ, compression and reverb only (no delay), a CI-only check with DiffVox's Python, one Windows build at the end. D-047, D-048.*

## What was built

- **Licence check** (step 0): DiffVox (github.com/SonyResearch/diffvox) is MIT-licensed as a whole and ships its preset datasets in `presets/` with no separate data licence. Only effect settings are used; the MIT notice ships with them.
- **Converter** `tools/diffvox/convert_presets.py` (numpy, run once by a developer, not shipped):
  1. decodes the 455 presets (Internal 385, MedleyDB 70) into Hz, dB and ms;
  2. clusters them into 10 groups and keeps, per group, the preset nearest the centre that MusicDAWM can reproduce;
  3. fits x42-fil4 to the preset's EQ on a model of fil4's own filters, maps the compressor to ACE Compressor and the reverb to a Space amount;
  4. computes, with DiffVox's own effects, what each preset does to a synthetic test vocal (EQ response, loudness, band balance, reverb level), written into the preset file for the tests.
- **Presets** `share/finalmix/vocal-presets.xml`: 10 presets with generated names (tone, compression, reverb).
- **Final Mix**: a **Vocal preset** menu in the FX panel of Vocals and Backing vocals tracks; one undo step; Brightness, Punch and Space work from the preset; the compressor threshold follows the track's loudness.
- **CI**: `tools/diffvox/check_decode.py` runs DiffVox's PyTorch model on all 455 presets in the Linux workflow and compares its decoded values with the converter's.

## Tests

`session_utils/final_mix_test.cc`, on the real plug-ins (x42-fil4, ACE Compressor, ACE Reverb):

| Check | Result |
|---|---|
| The 10 presets load | pass |
| The test vocal is the converter's (−18 LUFS) | −17.97 LUFS |
| Space bus reverb gain (the constant the presets use, −4.1 dB) | −4.07 dB |
| Each preset: fil4's measured EQ against DiffVox's curve (30 Hz – 16 kHz, where not cut by more than 20 dB) | 0.3 – 1.0 dB, all within 1.5 dB |
| Each preset on a track through Final Mix (EQ + compressor), exported: loudness against DiffVox's chain | within 0.6 LU |
| … band balance (5 bands above 80 Hz) | within 1.0 dB |
| … reverb level through the Space send against DiffVox's FDN | within 0.1 dB (by construction, with the measured bus gain) |
| Apply, none, undo | pass |

In the program (Xvfb): the menu appears for a Vocals track, lists the 10 presets, and choosing one updates the high-pass, Punch (50 %) and Space.

## Known limits

- The presets are rebuilt with MusicDAWM's effects: the reverb is the shared Space reverb (its amount follows the preset, not its room and colour), and DiffVox's expander and delay are left out.
- fil4's low-pass is not used; above 16 kHz the presets may differ from DiffVox.
- The test vocal is synthetic; the checks prove the translation, not the taste: **the user's listening round decides which presets stay and how they are named.**

## Acceptance test (for the user)

On a vocal of yours (role Vocals), try 3–4 presets with **Before / After**: does each sound like its name? Which are too much or useless?
